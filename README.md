## 🌟 Overview

**GPT-TYPE** is a modern, ultra-fast, and 100% offline desktop typing software engineered for Windows. Benchmarked against industry leaders like **Monkeytype**, **Keybr**, and **DOS Typeshala**, GPT-TYPE combines rigorous pedagogical touch-typing drills with a thrilling retro-arcade combat game and support for **over 123 world languages and scripts**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌍 1. 123+ World Languages & Scripts
- Full native Unicode character sets and authentic vocabulary drills for **123+ languages**, including English, Nepali, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and dozens of regional scripts.
- Zero external font downloads required — bundled with full fallback rendering for all complex Devanagari and non-Latin ligatures.

### 🇳🇵 2. Authentic Heritage Nepali Typeshala
- **Pure Preeti Font Engine**: Type in authentic legacy Preeti font layout or modern Devanagari Unicode.
- **Interactive Dual-Hand Guide**: Glowing SVG hands dynamically show you the exact finger to press for every character, fostering correct touch-typing muscle memory.
- **5 Practice Tiers**: Home Row, Top Row, Bottom Row, Number Row, and Full Complex Sentences.

### 🏹 3. 5-Level Ramayan Archery Battle (Arcade Game)
Defend the sacred *Lakshman Rekha* by typing falling demon arrows before they breach your defenses!
- **Level 1**: Shurpanakha's Enchanted Jungle (Speed: 1.0x)
- **Level 2**: Khardushan's Asura Garrison (Speed: 1.25x)
- **Level 3**: Kumbhakaran's Colossal Awakening (Speed: 1.55x)
- **Level 4**: Meghnad's Cloud Citadel - *Lightning Arrows* (Speed: 1.85x)
- **Level 5 (Final Boss)**: 10-Headed King Raavan (*Brahmastra Unleashed*) (Speed: 2.2x)
- **Combat Effects**: Floating score popups (`+100`, `+300 👑 BOSS!`), combo streak multipliers, and screen-shake rumble.

### 📈 4. Monkeytype-Style Visual WPM & Error Timeline Chart
- Second-by-second telemetry records your exact speed and mistakes as you type.
- Smooth cubic bezier spline line with luminous gradient fill.
- Red scatter markers (`×1`, `×2`) showing the exact second errors occurred.

### 🎯 5. Rhythm Consistency Score (%)
- Measures typing stability and rhythm based on keystroke pace standard deviation:
  $$\text{Consistency} = \max\left(25, 100 - \frac{\sigma}{\mu} \times 100\right)$$

### 🔊 6. Procedural Mechanical Switch Synthesizer (0ms Latency)
Built natively via the Web Audio API with zero audio lag:
- ⌨️ **Mechanical Thock**: Deep, resonant Holy Panda / Gateron Ink switch sound.
- 🔘 **Crisp Click**: Tactile Cherry MX Blue clickbar.
- 📜 **Vintage Typewriter**: Authentic acoustic metallic striker.
- 👾 **8-Bit Arcade Blip**: Retro chip sound.
- 🔉 **Soft Pop**: Subtle bubble pop.
- 🔇 **Mute Toggle**: Instant silent mode.

### 🎨 7. 7 Pro Color Themes
- **Dark Slate** (Default high-contrast modern slate)
- **Clean Light** (Daylight paper reading)
- **Cyberpunk Neon** (Neon magenta `#ff007f` & electric cyan `#00f2fe`)
- **Matrix Green** (Retro hacker green `#00ff66` on obsidian)
- **Dracula Purple** (Developer-favorite purple & pink)
- **Nord Arctic** (Clean Nordic ice frost `#88c0d0`)
- **Vintage Sepia** (Warm parchment paper `#fbf1c7`)

### 🧘 8. Pro Modes & Modifiers
- **Modes**: `⏱️ Time`, `📝 Words`, `📜 Literature Quotes`, and `🧘 Zen Mode` (infinite, pressure-free flow typing).
- **Modifiers**:
  - `@ punct`: Injects realistic commas, periods, quotation marks, and question marks.
  - `123 num`: Injects numbers into word drills.
  - `💀 master`: Sudden Death mode — test terminates immediately on your 1st typo!

### 🖼️ 9. 1-Click Social Scorecard Sharing
- **Copy Scorecard**: Formatted text ready to paste on Discord, Twitter, WhatsApp, Reddit, or Telegram.
- **Save Image**: Dynamically generates and downloads a **1200×630 high-definition social card image** (`GPT-TYPE-Scorecard.png`) featuring your WPM, accuracy, consistency, language, and speed chart.
