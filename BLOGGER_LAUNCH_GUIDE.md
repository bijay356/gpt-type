## 📋 How to Update Your Live Blogger Website

To apply these updates to your live site (`https://expermentweb.blogspot.com`):

### Step 1: Open Blogger Dashboard
1. Go to **https://www.blogger.com** and log in.
2. Select your blog from the top-left dropdown.

---

### Step 2: Paste the Updated Theme XML
1. In the left sidebar, click **Theme**.
2. Next to the orange 'CUSTOMIZE' button, click the **down arrow (▾)**:
   ```text
   Blogger Dashboard
     └── Theme
           └── Click down arrow (▾) next to "CUSTOMIZE"
                 ├── Backup
                 ├── Restore               ❌ (Do not click this)
                 ├── Switch to classic...
                 └── Edit HTML             ✅ (Click THIS)
   ```
3. Click **Edit HTML**.
4. Clear the existing code completely:
   - Click inside the editor box and press **Ctrl + A** (select all).
   - Press **Delete** or **Backspace**.
5. Open the file **`f:\blogger web\typeshala-blogger-theme.xml`** in Notepad or your editor:
   - Press **Ctrl + A** (select all) and **Ctrl + C** (copy).
6. Paste into the Blogger Edit HTML box (**Ctrl + V**).
7. Click the **Save icon** (floppy disk icon in the top-right corner).

---

### Step 3: Verify Desktop Mobile Setting
1. In **Theme**, click the **down arrow (▾)** next to 'CUSTOMIZE'.
2. Click **Mobile settings**.
3. Ensure **Desktop** is selected so mobile and tablet devices receive the responsive layout.
4. Click **Save**.

---

### Step 4: Test Your Live Site!
1. Open **https://expermentweb.blogspot.com** (or click **View Blog**).
2. Look at **Speed Test**:
   - Scroll through the Time options: Click **300s (5m)**, **600s (10m)**, or **∞ Unlimited**!
   - Click **Words**: Scroll through **100**, **200**, **500**, **1000**, or **∞ Unlimited**!
3. Look at the top navigation bar:
   - Click **📄 Pages ▾**: Open **About Us**, **Privacy Policy**, **Disclaimer**, or **Contact Us**!
4. In Blogger Dashboard ➔ **Layout**:
   - You can now add and customize widgets directly!

---

### Step 5: How to Add Google AdSense (100% Policy-Safe & Zero CLS)
Your new theme comes with **pre-configured AdSense layout sections**:
1. Go to **Blogger Dashboard ➔ Layout**.
2. You will see two dedicated ad sections:
   - **`Header Advertisement (AdSense 728x90 / Responsive)`** (Above the typing app)
   - **`Footer Advertisement (AdSense Responsive / Leaderboard)`** (Below the typing app)
3. Click **"Add a Gadget"** inside either section:
   - Option A: Select **"AdSense"** (if your Blogger account is linked to AdSense).
   - Option B: Select **"HTML/JavaScript"** and paste your Google AdSense unit code (`<ins class="adsbygoogle" ...></ins><script>(adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({});</script>`).
4. Click **Save**.
5. **Zero Layout Shift Feature**: Whenever an ad section has no active ads, it automatically collapses to `0px` (`display: none !important`), so your site remains completely clean and stable with no empty blank spaces!
