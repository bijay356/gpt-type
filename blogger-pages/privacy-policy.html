Privacy Policy for GPT-TYPE

Effective Date: September 2026  |  Website: https://gpttype.blogspot.com

1. Introduction & Core Privacy Principle

At GPT-TYPE, accessible from https://gpttype.blogspot.com, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that is collected, recorded, and utilized by GPT-TYPE.

Our fundamental guarantee: GPT-TYPE is built client-side. We do not record, intercept, log, or transmit your keystrokes, practice texts, or typing test inputs. All calculation of Words Per Minute (WPM), accuracy percentages, and game physics runs entirely within your device's browser memory.

2. Information We Collect

A. Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

To provide a smooth user experience, GPT-TYPE uses your browser's local storage to remember your personal interface preferences:

  • Your selected color theme (Dark, Light, Cyberpunk, Matrix, etc.)
  • Your sound effects toggle and volume preferences
  • Your highest scores in the Ramayan Archery battle and Car Game
  • Your selected language and typing test duration preferences

This data is stored purely on your local machine and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

B. Standard Log Files

Like most standard websites, Blogger and Google host servers log basic access information (IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks). These are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are used solely for analyzing traffic trends and administering the site.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under regulations such as GDPR (for European Union users) and CCPA (for California residents), users have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of categories of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of any personal data stored about them.
  • Request that a business not sell their personal data (we never sell user data).

Because GPT-TYPE does not create accounts or store user identities in database servers, you can instantly erase all local data by simply clearing your browser cache and cookies.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children online is especially important to us. GPT-TYPE is a safe educational resource and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
