About Us — GPT-TYPE

Welcome to GPT-TYPE — the premier free, multilingual typing speed test, classic touch-typing tutor, and interactive keyboard arcade platform designed to empower learners, developers, government job aspirants, students, and typing enthusiasts worldwide.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is straightforward: to make touch-typing education freely accessible, highly engaging, and culturally inclusive for everyone across the globe.

While many typing platforms cater only to English, GPT-TYPE breaks linguistic barriers by natively supporting 123+ world languages and scripts alongside specialized heritage keyboards like authentic Nepali Preeti and Unicode Devanagari.

⚡ What We Offer

🌐 123+ Languages Speed Test

Test typing speed in Nepali (Preeti & Romanized Unicode), Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Chinese, Russian, Japanese Romaji, and code languages with real-time WPM, CPM, and accuracy telemetry.

📚 Authentic Classic Typeshala

Preserving the legendary DOS Typeshala touch-typing curriculum with interactive dual-hand SVG finger guides, home-row progression, and authentic keyboard layouts.

🏹 5-Level Ramayan Battle

An action typing game where you defeat demons (Shurpanakha, Khardushan, Kumbhakaran, Meghnad, Raavan) with typing reflex accuracy across all 122 languages.

🏎️ Turbo Grand Prix Car Game

Compete on asphalt racing tracks against AI opponent cars with dynamic scenery, bridge landmarks, tunnel passages, and nitro speed boosts.

🔒 Privacy & Offline Standalone Guarantee

At GPT-TYPE, we respect your privacy completely. 100% of your typing inputs, keystrokes, and speed calculations happen client-side in your local browser memory. We never transmit, store, or monitor what you type.

Furthermore, our full platform is available as a lightweight, 100% offline Windows desktop application that requires zero internet connection after download.
